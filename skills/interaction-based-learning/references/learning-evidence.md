# Learning evidence and limits

Use these sources to choose a technique and explain why it fits the objective. Evidence for one task or outcome may generalize only under similar conditions. Treat a proposed tutoring rule or artefact feature as a design choice unless it was evaluated directly.

| Technique | Use and limits | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Retrieval and feedback | Ask the learner to recall, explain or solve without the answer visible. Correct errors and give information that helps with the next attempt. For transfer, practice identifying and applying the principle across examples and assess with a new example. | [Dunlosky et al. (2013)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26173288/), [Rowland (2014)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25150680/), [Pan & Rickard (2018)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29733621/), [Wisniewski et al. (2020)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32038429/) |
| Spacing and successive relearning | Retrieve with feedback to a criterion and revisit on later days. The useful gap depends on retention horizon and task. Cepeda et al. examined factual material with a review gap followed by a later test. Their results support adapting gaps, with no universal sequence of dates. | [Cepeda et al. (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02209.x), [Rawson & Dunlosky (2022)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/09637214221100484) |
| Worked examples and fading | Give more guidance when the learner lacks task-specific knowledge. Move from an example to a completion problem and then to independent practice as the learner gains competence. Segment instruction when the learner cannot yet coordinate its steps. Cowan’s approximate chunk estimate comes from particular short-term memory tasks and provides no fixed cap on instructional steps. | [Kalyuga et al. (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_4), [Cowan (2001)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11515286/) |
| Prequestioning and productive failure | Use a brief prequestion to focus attention or diagnose prior knowledge. Check understanding of important content beyond the prequestions. For productive failure, choose a problem with plausible entry points, then compare the learner’s strategies during explicit consolidation. The overall problem-solving-first comparison depends on the task, implementation and population. | [Pan & Carpenter (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09814-5), [Sinha & Kapur (2021)](https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543211019105) |
| Interleaving | Mix confusable categories or methods when discrimination is an objective. Give enough guidance for the learner to recognize the features that distinguish them. Kornell and Bjork studied artist classification from paintings. The small, heterogeneous set of word-based studies in Brunmair and Richter does not establish a rule for all verbal material. | [Kornell & Bjork (2008)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18578849/), [Brunmair & Richter (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209) |
| Self-explanation and interactive visuals | Ask the learner to explain why a step works, which assumption changed, or how a manipulated variable affected the visual. Evaluate the substance of the explanation. Design the interaction to let the learner investigate a relationship in the material. | [Bisra et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-018-9434-x), [de Jong et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1230579) |
| Learning and confidence | Distinguish current assisted performance, immediate unaided performance, delayed retention and transfer. Confidence in a submitted answer and a prediction of later recall measure different judgments. Delayed recall predictions can improve the ranking of items by likelihood of recall while their absolute calibration depends on the task and measurement. | [Soderstrom & Bjork (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691615569000), [Nelson & Dunlosky (1991)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.1991.tb00147.x), [Van Overschelde & Nelson (2006)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17263076/) |
| AI-supported practice | In Bastani et al.’s high-school mathematics trial, teachers reviewed topics and worked examples before AI-assisted practice. Standard GPT-4 improved practice grades and reduced subsequent unaided exam grades relative to control. The tutor condition, which bundled teacher-designed hints and other prompt inputs, largely removed the harm without a demonstrated exam gain. Use this as a reason to include independent practice and assessment. The trial did not isolate a universal rule about withholding answers. | [Bastani et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) |

For planning, a specific if–then cue can support follow-through. Keep the plan tied to an actual retrieval or application task. [Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1)

Check the original source before quoting a numerical result. Give the comparison and outcome, and include the strongest limitation that affects the learner’s situation. Avoid treating preferences for a learning style, a fixed attention span or a specific interval timer as established learning mechanisms.
